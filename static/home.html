BAUBAU Alert 的用途

BAUBAU Alert 是一個為 VTuber 粉絲社群打造的 Discord 機器人，提供各種通知，並可透過驗證 YouTube 頻道會員資格來授予 Discord 會限身分組。它運作於已將其加入的 Discord 伺服器中，沒有獨立的網站登入或消費者帳號。

📣 通知功能

  • 關鍵字通知 — 當你訂閱的關鍵字出現在頻道時以私訊提醒你。
  • 追蹤 YouTube 頻道的開台與新影片通知（透過 Holodex）。
  • 追蹤的 YouTube 社群貼文的新貼文通知。

✅ YouTube 會員驗證（使用 Google 登入）

伺服器管理員可將某個 YouTube 頻道對應到一個 Discord 身分組。成員點選 /verify_membership 並以 Google 登入，授予 BAUBAU Alert YouTube 存取權（youtube.force-ssl）。

BAUBAU Alert 僅用此權限確認你能否觀看該頻道的會限內容（即你是否為付費會員），若是則授予對應的 Discord 身分組，並定期重新檢查，會員資格結束時移除身分組。

因 YouTube API 設計問題，本服務僅能透過該權限來讀取會限內容，進行確認是否為付費會員，本服務不會存取你的影片、觀看紀錄、訂閱，也不會代你發布或修改任何內容。你可隨時以 /membership_unlink 或於 Google 帳戶權限解除連結。詳見隱私權政策（含 Google API 服務有限使用聲明）。

What BAUBAU Alert does

BAUBAU Alert is a Discord bot for VTuber fan communities. It delivers notifications and can grant members-only Discord roles by verifying YouTube channel membership. It runs inside Discord servers that have added it; there is no separate consumer account or website login.

📣 Notifications

  • Keyword alerts — DMs you when a keyword you subscribed to appears in a channel.
  • Live-stream and video notifications for followed YouTube channels (via Holodex).
  • New posts from followed YouTube community tabs.

✅ YouTube membership verification (uses Google Sign-In)

A server admin can link a YouTube channel to a Discord role. A member then clicks /verify_membership and signs in with Google, granting BAUBAU Alert YouTube access (youtube.force-ssl).

BAUBAU Alert uses that access only to check whether the member can view the channel's members-only content — i.e. whether they are a paying channel member — and, if so, grants the matching Discord role. It re-checks periodically and removes the role when the membership ends.

Because of how the YouTube Data API works, this scope is the only way the Service can read that members-only content to confirm whether you are a paying member. It does not access your videos, watch history, subscriptions, or post/modify anything on your behalf. You can unlink anytime with /membership_unlink or at Google account permissions.
